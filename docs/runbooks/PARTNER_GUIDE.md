# 🏋️ Paradise Gym — Partner Operations Cheat Sheet

> **Audience:** Gym Managing Partners & Front-Desk Staff  
> **Purpose:** Step-by-step operating guide for daily gym routines, registrations, payments, and attendance.

---

## 1. Daily Routine

### Morning Shift (Opening)
1. Open the **Paradise Gym PWA** on the reception phone or tablet.
2. Check the **Dashboard**:
   - Verify **Active Members** and **Expiring in 3 Days** count.
   - Look at **Outstanding Dues** to follow up with members arriving today.
3. Tap **Attendance** -> **Start Camera Scanner** to mount the device for member QR scanning.

### Evening Shift (Closing & Cash Reconciliation)
1. Navigate to **Dashboard** -> **Financial Overview**.
2. Note today's **Cash Collections** vs **UPI Collections**.
3. Physically count the cash drawer and ensure it matches the **Cash Collections** figure in the PWA.
4. Record any daily petty cash expenses (e.g. cleaning supplies, drinking water) under **More -> Operating Expenses**.
5. If taking a personal partner withdrawal, record it under **More -> Partner Capital & Draws** as a **Debit (Personal Draw)**.

---

## 2. Managing Members & Approvals

### How to Approve a New Self-Registered Member:
1. Member scans the gym entrance QR code on their own smartphone and fills in their name, phone, and emergency contact.
2. The registration instantly arrives in your **More -> Public Registration Queue**.
3. Tap **Review**:
   - Check their name and phone number.
   - Tap **Approve & Assign Plan** -> Select Plan (e.g. Monthly Standard ₹1,500) -> Record Initial Payment.
   - The member is now active, assigned an ID card, and ready to check in!

### How to Share a Digital ID Card:
1. Tap **Members** -> Find member name.
2. Tap the member profile -> **Digital Card**.
3. Tap **Share on WhatsApp** to send them their personalized card with their unique QR check-in token.

---

## 3. Payments, Renewals & Split Collections

### Taking a Payment with Multiple Methods:
1. In the **Record Payment** modal:
   - If member pays partly in Cash and partly via UPI (e.g., ₹500 Cash + ₹1,000 UPI):
   - Select **Split Payment**.
   - Input ₹500 in Cash and ₹1,000 in UPI.
   - Both transactions are logged atomically with the same receipt number.
2. Tap **Send WhatsApp Receipt** to send the member an official receipt link with zero paper waste.

### Handling Early Renewals:
- If a member's plan expires on Dec 20, but they pay to renew on Dec 10:
- The system **automatically preserves the remaining 10 days**. The new plan will start on Dec 20 and expire on Jan 19.

---

## 4. Handling Offline Internet Outages

1. If the gym Wi-Fi drops or mobile cellular network is down, the PWA will display an **"Offline Mode"** badge at the top.
2. **DO NOT PANIC.** Keep scanning member QR codes normally.
3. Every check-in is saved in the device's secure local memory.
4. Once internet returns, the app will automatically upload all check-ins to the cloud.

---

## 5. Holidays & Gym Closures

1. When the gym is closing for festivals (e.g., Diwali) or AC servicing:
2. Go to **More -> Gym Closures & Extensions**.
3. Tap **Record Closure**:
   - Set Start Date and End Date.
   - Check **"Automatically extend active memberships"**.
   - Tap **Confirm Closure**.
4. All active members automatically get extra days added to their membership expiry, keeping members happy and relationships strong!
